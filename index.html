01 — the route

One grand loop

Christchurch to Christchurch, clockwise — and the direction is the whole trick. The new moon falls on 9 November, so the loop runs to the Mackenzie first: Mt John on the 6th, the free camp on Lake Pukaki on the 8th, both inside the darkest window of the trip. From there over Lindis Pass to Queenstown, deep into Fiordland, north through the lakes and Haast to glacier country, up the wild West Coast, over Lewis Pass, and home along the whale coast. No backtracking that doesn't earn its keep.
